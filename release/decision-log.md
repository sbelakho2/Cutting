## 2026-10-29 — Phase C: sensor/material-state observability and handling

- **Architecture**: kept `sim_board.hpp` byte-identical to the pinned fixture (its motor-coupled encoders are the preserved counterexample's subject) and added `software/machine/tests/material_plant.hpp`, an independent material plant: bodies (stock/piece/stub/slug), station occupancy (W0 X=-385, W1 X=-205, D X=106.75), counts generated only from material motion at occupied stations, real grip limits (a severed body shorter than the far right nip at 140 mm is never propelled), takeaway slide, and waste-chute fall physics. The plant receives only actuator commands; fault injections are explicit.
- **Controller observability (M27)**: tracked feeds now carry a physical observability plan. Stock moves verify against the two stock wheels (both are stock-side; their agreement is redundancy, never absolute proof). Piece moves verify against station D only within the piece's occupancy window (`piece_station_observed_um`), signed and occupancy-bounded; motion outside the window is a `wire_geometry` fault, never accepted. The ejection's unobserved remainder (for a 300 mm piece at tail X3, D sees 103.75 mm of the 230 mm move) is legal only for the final ejection and requires the discharge present->clear evidence before acceptance. Rejected alternative: keeping the legacy encoder[side]==motor[side] mapping — that is exactly the counterexample defect.
- **Prime stub handling (M30)**: the 10 mm stub cannot reach the nearest right nip (75.5 mm) and is no longer "ejected" by feed motion. It is removed by the constrained mechanical sweep (`Outputs::waste_sweep`) and verified by a waste-chute present->clear transit before any further feed/blade close. Strip slugs get the same chain at LeadOpen/TailOpen. Blocked sweep faults `waste_not_cleared` with the body still at the pocket (tested).
- **Interrupted runs**: the wire-body model is no longer blindly reset at `begin_job_run`. A leftover severed piece is scrap-discharged first; the prime pass classifies the severed body by its REAL length (short -> stub sweep; long -> scrap discharge, uncounted because the interruption already counted the scrap). The model resets only at an explicit rethread (`home()`), per M37 recovery semantics.
- **Discharge evidence fix**: edge history is captured from the start of the ejection window and never restarted at PieceDone; a body already presenting at window start counts as present evidence. Early-clear parts are accepted (the old code missed the only transition); stuck-clear/stuck-present sensors fault and quarantine (tested).
- **Production gating**: `part_clear_check` defaults to 1 and can only be disabled in explicit RAM-only service fixture mode (`set_fixture_mode` / `Msg::SetFixtureMode`). Fixture runs never emit accepted counts (`pieces_fixture`), never persist approval, and `approve()` refuses in fixture mode. Leaving fixture mode restores the production default.
- **Layout changes (deliberate, reviewed)**: `Settings` v2 — `counts_per_metre[3]`, `wheel_sign[3]` (W0/W1/D), `reserved[4]`, sizeof 72 (was 64); journal `Record` 84 (was 76); protocol revision 7: `status_len` 124 (adds piece counts, `pieces_fixture`, piece calibration, fixture/part-clear state), `calibration_len` 12, input bit 13 `waste_clear`, output bit 5 `waste_sweep`, `Msg::SetFixtureMode`. `set_calibration` takes all three stations.
- **Target adapter**: `board_rp2350.cpp` no longer hardcodes `part_clear = true` (the handoff's bypass). The unassigned discharge/waste channels FAIL CLOSED (read "not clear"); `waste_sweep` is intentionally undriven. Pin assignment is the Phase-H I/O gate, recorded in `requirements/signal-table.md`.
- **Legacy constants (M01)**: `machine_spec.hpp` 3200 pulses/rev and 40 kHz ceiling are named `legacy_*`, quarantined to `software/firmware`, and statically asserted distinguishable from the active production scale (2000 pulses/rev, 8 kHz).
- **Counterexample tool on the revised tree**: `reproduce_stock_side_mismatch.py` reports COUNTEREXAMPLE_NOT_REPRODUCED (exit 5) because the revised controller faults at the handling gate (0x80000) on the legacy adapted fixture rather than reaching the ejection-length signature — with `pieces_accepted=0` either way. Per the tool's own guard (narrow expectations, review before applying to materially changed code) it is not modified; the pinned reproduction at `tests/reproduction-2026-10-29/stock-side-counterexample/` stays frozen and the in-tree `negative_control` group is the maintained regression. Recorded in `tests/reproduction-2026-10-29/revised-tree/README.md`.
- **Eject grip limit (surfaced by the plant, feeds M30/M47)**: right-cassette grip ends when the piece tail passes the far nip at X140, so only ~137 mm of the commanded 230 mm ejection is driven; the remainder is the designed takeaway slide. The takeaway mechanism (guided tray/conveyor, force, speed, 3 m support) is a Phase-G/M30 design gate; the plant models the slide at 500 mm/s pending that design.
