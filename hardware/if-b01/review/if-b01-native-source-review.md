# IF-B01 native source review record

**Revision:** `S1-B-IF-B01-NATIVE-DRAFT-R2-2026-09-13`  
**Status:** source-level evidence only — **not released for PCB fabrication,
assembly, energisation, cabinet integration, safety use or machine operation.**

This record identifies what the native KiCad baseline proves and, importantly,
what it does not prove. It is not a design-review approval, manufacturing
release, physical test result or safety validation record.

## Scope of the native baseline

The editable [project](../if-b01.kicad_pro), [logical schematic](../if-b01.kicad_sch),
[local symbols](../if-b01.kicad_sym), [local footprints](../if-b01.pretty) and
[intent manifest](../if-b01-native-intent.json) are generated from the controlled
[component input](../if-b01-bom.csv), [connector-net input](../if-b01-netlist.csv)
and [controller signal specification](../../../shared/controls_spec.hpp).

The schematic embeds its local-symbol cache so it remains parseable without a
user-specific symbol-library configuration. The PCB file is a
$750\times400\ \mathrm{mm}$, four-layer placed/routed draft with all 90 board
footprints, four M3 mounting holes, local F.Cu pad escapes, In1.Cu vertical
breakouts and B.Cu net buses. The intentionally spacious routing-cell strategy
makes all generated copper inspectable, but remains subject to human and
fabricator review.

## Preserved source constraints

- U2 channels 1–7 are BF `PUL−`, BF `DIR−`, BR `PUL−`, BR `DIR−`, FL `PUL−`,
  FL `DIR−`, and FR `PUL−`; U3 channel 1 is FR `DIR−`.
- U3 channels 6 and 7 inputs are tied to `0V_PELV`; their outputs have explicit
  no-connects and no field connector allocation.
- U1 receives the four encoder differential pairs and exposes only the selected
  non-inverted receiver outputs to `GP8`–`GP11`.
- C1 is the 16 V 3.3 V receiver decoupler. C2/C3 are the distinct 50 V
  `GRM188R71H104KA93D` bypass capacitors from `+24V_TPL_COM` to `0V_PELV`.
- The common-anode 24 V PUL/DIR/ENA positive wiring remains cabinet wiring and
  is not routed through IF-B01.
