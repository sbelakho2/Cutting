Verification and release
python3 tools/run_cabinet_review.py --sanitize compiles and runs thermal/reference/fault-state
checks and the current machine host regressions. Its result files identify actual success and always retain
physical_results=false, target_rp2350_tested=false. A host pass does not prove real relay timing, sensor
response, position accuracy or cut quality. Full CAD/FEM regeneration, KiCad manufacturing release,
target cross-build/pulse capture and original length/strip/Cpk/load/fit/endurance/safety tests remain
separate required scopes. A failed physical or supplier gate cannot be closed by writing a model pass.
The current build cannot honestly be guaranteed 100% functional, globally cheapest, or 70%
cheaper as a complete delivered machine. The reviewed change set and explicit closure records
make the remaining work testable without weakening the goals.

Use two STEPPERONLINE CL86Y-V20 head drivers and two CL57Y-V20 feed drivers. Keep the 34E1K-85
head motors and 24HS40-5004D-E1000 feed motors. Both driver manuals specify 0-50°C operating
ambient. Head continuous current is 6 Arms; feed is 5.6 Arms, covering the original 5.7 Arms
torque-curve point and 5 A feed motor. Hot torque, tuning and position error require qualification. The
head manual's voltage table and prose disagree: use 80 VDC as the conservative ceiling, with nominal
60 V and a separately qualified regeneration clamp.
Install two Pfannenberg PF66000 IP54, 11666102055, each paired with one PFA60000 IP54,
11760002055. At 230 V/50 Hz, the official matched-path flow is 445 m³/h, nominal input 120 W per fan,
6 A prefuse, ball-bearing L10 40,000 h at 40°C. Do not use the 640 m³/h free-air figure. Two clean parallel
paths have a catalogue total of 890 m³/h; the actual installed combined flow must be at least 800
m³/h. The maker's web page says G3 while its current sheet says G4: order the exact matched IP54 filter
identity and reconcile supplied mats before assembly.
Replace control supplies with MEAN WELL HDR-60-24, 24 V/2.5 A/60 W, and MDR-40-5, 5 V/6 A/30 W.
MDR-40 has a DC-OK dry contact; the cheaper MDR-20's active 20 mA signal is unsuitable for the selected
permission circuit. At the 5.2 V setting, output must remain below 30/5.2 = 5.77 A and the temperature
derating curve. Verify RU3100 loaded supply ≥4.75 V and Pico VSYS ≤5.5 V, including tolerance, ripple
and transients. Keep 24 V driver logic and TPL suppression COM; do not convert them to 5 V.
Use Rittal SK3114.200 / 3114200 on protected 24 VDC, with its supplied 1.8 m NTC. Set menu 6=2 (K2
alarm), menu 8=2°C (low alarm), menu 9=45°C (high alarm). K2 healthy NO 11-14 opens on temperature
alarm and sensor open/short. Catalogue switching/display accuracy is ±2 K; alarm hysteresis is 2 K. K1 is
unused. Fans run continuously after the main isolator, upstream of the motor-power contactors.
Additional required parts: two fan fuse holders/links, spare mats, two MF adapter relays, two thermal
permission relays, three protected drive-logic branch-presence relays, two airflow monitors, separated
terminals/barriers, PE bonding, conductors/ferrules, mounting and a checked enclosure arrangement. An
unpriced installation lot does not select every discrete item. See thermal BOM.

GPIO field-interface minimum load
The present 10kΩ series /47kΩ pull-up circuit draws 3.3/(10000+47000) = 57.9µA at 3.3V when the
isolated contact closes. The selected Phoenix 2966171 power-contact relay has a 10mA at 12V
minimum switching load. This is an inadequate specified load. Switching reliability/endurance cannot
be inferred from a new relay initially conducting on the bench. Even gold 2966265 is specified at 1mA/24V
and 100mV/10mA; do not assume a57.9µA loop is qualified by changing the plating alone.
Replace KA1-KA10 with a qualified low-current interface before release. An economical candidate is a
Panasonic AQY212GS PhotoMOS channel: manufacturer maximum on resistance 0.7Ω, off leakage 1µA,
LED operate current 3mA, recommended 5mA input and 48V load ceiling. A3.3kΩ1% resistor from
a19.2-28.8V field input gives at least 5.31mA using the 1.5V LED upper drop, and at most 8.82mA using
zero drop as a conservative bound. Worst resistor heat is0.254W; use a properly derated ≥0.5W part. Add
defined polarity/surge protection, connectors, PCB/insulation/mounting and fixture tests. This is a circuit
candidate, not a released or completely priced module. GPIO loading, silicon leakage and actual on/off
logic margins must be recomputed together; do not replace a DIN relay with an IC alone.
Maker reference: AQY212GS specifications. Asian pricing reference: LCSC C719745. The physical input
adapter, EMI/cold/hot/fault tests and incoming MCU silicon revision remain open. High-cycle home sensing
also needs an endurance count. KSR1/XPS-reset minimum load must be measured too. R2 and
driver/thermal permission relays serve other loads; do not blanket-remove them.

Mains, regeneration and fault coordination
The archived LE350 sheets specify40A cold inrush each at 230VAC. Three supplies can therefore
contribute 120A before the new control supplies and fans. Retire the old 4.6A incoming figure.
Measure/obtain real RMS current, power factor and cold/hot-start waveforms for the complete cabinet. A
nominal C10 breaker or6A fan link is not a coordination proof; choose exact protection from the site fault

regeneration headroom.
