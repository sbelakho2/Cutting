# P00-P15 physical test procedures

Generated by `tools/generate_ph_tests.py`. Each test is executable as
written on the physical machine/bench. Record templates in `records/`
ship `executed: false`; physical execution is the hardware gate (D8).
Acceptance criteria follow the requirements register and handoff 2/11;
they may only be relaxed by an approved requirement change, never by a
failed run.

## Rigor requirements (apply to every test in this harness)

**Instruments and traceability.** Every instrument carries a current
ISO/IEC 17025-traceable (or national-equivalent) calibration certificate
dated before the test; the record lists instrument identity, certificate
reference and stated uncertainty. Where a measurand decides acceptance, a
gauge R&R study exists and the acceptance rule below is applied.

**Measurement uncertainty and conformity.** Every quantitative acceptance
uses the predeclared conformity rule `|error| + U <= T`, with U the
expanded uncertainty (k=2) of the measurement chain and T the requirement
half-width from the requirements register. U and T are recorded with the
result; a pass without U is not a result. Capability claims additionally
require the P13 statistical plan (see P13-T3).

**Sample sizes (minimum; increase where the plan demands).**
- capability cells (P13): >= 30 unique consecutive pieces per cell;
  failures and scrap retained in the dataset
- quality coupons (P07): >= 10 coupons per material x OD cell, spread
  across at least 3 blade-wear stages
- force/pressure points (P06): >= 5 readings per position and condition,
  reported individually (no averaged-only data)
- proof/structural loads (P05): full procedure on the assembled machine,
  plus instrumentation zero/check runs before and after
- electrical function (P02/P03/P11): >= 3 repetitions per case; life
  screenings at the declared operation count
- endurance (P14): the declared duty block; interruptions recorded
- safety validation (P12): per ISO 13849-2 with the competent engineer's
  documented procedure

**Predeclared analysis.** Acceptance thresholds and analysis methods are
fixed before data collection (they live in these procedures). A failed run
triggers a corrective design/process change and a fresh run; the failed
record stays in the dataset and the acceptance criteria are never edited
to fit data (handoff 11.1/14). Deviations from the procedure are logged in
the record with cause and effect assessment.

**Environment and data integrity.** Every record captures ambient
temperature, RH, mains voltage and relevant state (filters, doors, duty).
Raw data is retained at its recorded path with its hash; derived values
carry the calculation reference. Synthetic or simulated values are never
recorded as measured; simulations are labelled as models. Timestamps are
machine-generated.

**Independence and completion labels.** P15-T1 is executed by a competent
builder who did not design the machine. Test-completion records use only
the labels **BLOCKING_PASS** (passed for the declared scope with residual
actions tracked) or **CLOSED** (passed, no residual action) - the only
permitted completion labels (handoff 12.3).

## P00 receiving and evidence verification

### P00-T1 — Identity and certificate check of every received line

- Linked goals: G37, G38, G47 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M48
- Instruments: calibrated calipers/micrometer; document register
- Setup: All purchased and fabricated lines delivered to the receiving bench with their packing documents.
- Steps: 1) Match every delivered item to its BOM id and exact_order string; 2) verify manufacturer marks/certificates (material certs for custom steel/aluminium); 3) record serials/lot codes; 4) quarantine mismatches.
- Acceptance: Every delivered item matches its exact BOM identity; certificates present where the row claims them; mismatches quarantined, never substituted.

### P00-T2 — First-article dimensional inspection of custom parts

- Linked goals: G37, G38, G47 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M02;M45
- Instruments: CMM or height gauge + micrometer; surface plate
- Setup: First article of each custom family (H001-H007, F1xx, M2xx, P001-P008, AX parts).
- Steps: 1) Verify every controlled dimension on the released drawing; 2) record measured vs nominal with instrument id; 3) check critical fits (bearing pockets H7, 20 h6 spindle) individually; 4) record capability notes where the drawing demands them.
- Acceptance: All controlled dimensions within drawing tolerance; fit-critical dimensions measured with micrometre-class instruments; records include measurement uncertainty.

### P00-T3 — Price/evidence reconciliation of the purchase set

- Linked goals: G38, G39, G47 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M45;M48
- Instruments: invoice register; bom reconciliation tool
- Setup: Invoices/packing lists for the delivered set available.
- Steps: 1) Run tools/reconcile_bom.py; 2) match each invoice line to its BOM row and dated observation; 3) record the realised basket price at actual purchase quantity; 4) flag every deviation from the observation (price/MOQ/quantity).
- Acceptance: Every cost line has a dated source and matches its BOM occurrence; deviations recorded; blank never treated as zero (handoff 12.3).

### P00-T4 — MOQ/pack and spare-quantity verification

- Linked goals: G38, G47 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M48
- Instruments: count; scale
- Setup: Delivered quantities unpacked and counted.
- Steps: 1) Count delivered vs BOM quantity and declared MOQ/pack; 2) record spares received; 3) record any forced over-purchase and its unit-cost effect.
- Acceptance: Actual purchase quantity and unit cost recorded per line; over-purchase effects included in the cost rollup.

### P00-T5 — Conformance of fabricated assemblies to released drawings

- Linked goals: G37, G41 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M02;M45
- Instruments: drawing set; gauges
- Setup: Subassemblies from the fabrication supplier.
- Steps: 1) Check assembly identity against the drawing revision; 2) verify weld/heat-treatment records where stated; 3) verify surface finish/coating; 4) photograph and file the as-built state.
- Acceptance: As-built matches the released drawing revision; deviations require an approved change record before use.

## P01 fits and mechanical assembly

### P01-T1 — Bearing-seat interference measurement (matched pairs)

- Linked goals: G16, G17 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M03;M07;M22
- Instruments: micrometre (0.001 mm class); bore gauge; temperature log
- Setup: Housing/adapter seats and shaft journals at measurement temperature 20-25 C recorded.
- Steps: 1) Measure each seat and journal diameter at 3 positions x 2 directions; 2) compute actual interference per pair; 3) record the remaining bearing internal clearance envelope; 4) store measured pairs as the permanent record.
- Acceptance: Actual interference 3-6 um per pair with documented measurement uncertainty; CNC access is not accepted as 1 um capability; remaining bearing clearance inside the manufacturer envelope (G16).

### P01-T2 — Collar/keyway fit and torque-set record (F109)

- Linked goals: G19 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M05;M07
- Instruments: torque wrench (calibrated); feeler; marker
- Setup: Collar, key and shaft finish-inspected.
- Steps: 1) Assemble per instruction; 2) apply the specified clamp torque with calibrated wrench; 3) mark fastener positions; 4) run slip/settlement screening (static torque then reversal).
- Acceptance: No slip at 5 N.m service torque with the 7 kN class clamp recorded; settlement marks stable; fretting inspection after the screening (G19).

### P01-T3 — Screw/journal runout and end-machining conformance

- Linked goals: G11, G37 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M04;M06
- Instruments: dial indicator 0.001; V-blocks
- Setup: Finished screw on V-blocks.
- Steps: 1) Measure journal runout and diameter (8 h7 x 18 ends); 2) verify thread forms; 3) record straightness at mid-span.
- Acceptance: Journals within h7; runout within drawing; end machining matches the one frozen configuration (10 mm lead, 1000 pulses/rev).

### P01-T4 — Holder/tooling preload and fastener torque audit

- Linked goals: G22, G25 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M05;M08
- Instruments: calibrated torque wrench; angle gauge
- Setup: Head assembled with production fasteners (exact class/length from the manual).
- Steps: 1) Torque every fastener to the manual value (or record PENDING->resolved value); 2) verify thread engagement (15 mm nominal where stated); 3) re-check after a run-in cycle.
- Acceptance: Every fastener at its documented torque with engagement verified; no fastener left at a guessed value.

### P01-T5 — Coupling alignment and runout under rotation

- Linked goals: G25, G37 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M06
- Instruments: dial indicator; alignment tool
- Setup: Motor-coupling-screw train assembled.
- Steps: 1) Check angular/parallel alignment at the coupling; 2) rotate by hand through full travel; 3) record runout at the screw journal.
- Acceptance: Alignment within the coupling manufacturer's limits; no binding through full travel.

### P01-T6 — Rail/block preload and stage parallelism

- Linked goals: G14, G37 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M03;M04
- Instruments: dial indicator; granite/straight edge
- Setup: Rails mounted on the base plates.
- Steps: 1) Measure rail straightness and height match; 2) verify block preload class; 3) measure carriage parallelism across travel.
- Acceptance: Machined-section stiffness claim requires the measured stage geometry; parallelism within the drawing (G14 evidence input).

### P01-T7 — Assembly cleanliness and void inspection (cast/epoxy features)

- Linked goals: G20, G37 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M04;M05
- Instruments: borescope; scales
- Setup: Filled/finished structural parts.
- Steps: 1) Inspect filled regions for voids where the process includes them; 2) record mass vs nominal; 3) verify edge breaks/deburring.
- Acceptance: No void above the drawing limit; mass within process tolerance; no sharp edges at wire/handling paths.

## P02 protection bench

### P02-T2 — I2t and let-through measurement at fault current

- Linked goals: G32 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M32

- Setup: Bench fault path with metered energy.
- Steps: 1) Apply the declared fault case; 2) record let-through I2t; 3) compare with wire and terminal I2t limits.

### P02-T3 — PSU-limited fault clearing verification

- Acceptance: Every PSU-limited fault either clears the protection or is demonstrably limited below hazard; no nuisance trip in normal load steps.

### P02-T4 — Input vs output protection separation audit

- Linked goals: G32 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M33

- Setup: Cabinet energised-bench (safety isolation for checks).
- Steps: 1) Trace every branch to its protection device; 2) verify no common-mode path couples input and output protection; 3) verify selective coordination where required.
- Acceptance: Branch separation as designed; no protection device silently shared between the safety input and output sides.

### P02-T5 — Conductor sizing and terminal torque verification

- Linked goals: G32 &nbsp;|&nbsp; models: M32

- Setup: Production wiring samples.
- Steps: 1) Verify wire gauge/length per the released wiring schedule; 2) torque every terminal to its spec; 3) pull-test crimps sample-wise.
- Acceptance: Sizing and torque match the electrical release; crimps pass pull test; the release document is the authority (manual warns against guessed values).
