Approve a vector master before printing production decals; do not distort, mirror, or laser-cut load-bearing structure into the logo shape.

Minimum mark size on the board: 20 mm. Reserve clear space at least one quarter of mark height in this prototype layout pending the brand owner's exact X-unit definition. Use a 120 × 90 mm exterior decal; no branding obscures guards, warning labels, or indicators. Black structural skins, gray working surfaces, red non-safety accent strip. Emergency-stop devices retain their required red actuator/yellow background independently of brand colors.

The original artwork remains the owner's material. No Komax trademarks or product imagery are embedded in the STARZ machine.
