## Sensitivity studies

The companion sensitivity table sweeps actual motor-curve operating points, imposed wire/shear/wear loads, strand buckling, tension relaxation, nip traction, point-contact screening, thermal resistance, wheel phase/runout, bounded calibration error, bus capacitance/returned energy and dancer time step. Sweeps are **not multiple machine designs**. They expose which physical properties must be controlled or measured for the single design.

The contact sphere is a dimensional/nonlinearity screen only; it is not passed off as a crossed rubber-roller/insulated-wire Hertz solution. No no-damage conclusion follows from it. Archard wear is not used to invent a blade replacement interval.

3D FE studies were not requested in this run.
